Chapter 1: The Silver Guild

Eldrin adjusted his glasses and looked across the table at Lyra. At thirty-five years old, 
he was the youngest Master Alchemist in Oakhaven. 

"The Aetherium drive operates on strict principles, Lyra," Eldrin said softly. "It can only 
harness blue mana. If exposed to red mana, the core destabilizes immediately and explodes."

Lyra nodded, taking mental notes. Eldrin had never trusted her since the Incident at the Docks 
two years ago, where she accidentally burned his research notes.
